# Owner and Office Staff Requirements

## Overview

Owners and office staff manage tea collection records, deductions, payments, receipts, and reports. TeaRoutePay aims to reduce manual work, improve accounting accuracy, and support better business management.

---

# Office Staff Responsibilities

- Receive route books from collectors
- Enter collection records into the system
- Calculate monthly payments
- Manage deductions
- Generate receipts
- Maintain accounting records
- Prepare business reports

---

# Current Workflow Challenges

- Manual data entry
- Human calculation errors
- Time-consuming monthly processing
- Limited automation
- Limited reporting support
- Outdated desktop system interface

---

# Functional Requirements

## FR-01 – User Authentication
Office staff and owners should securely access the system.

## FR-02 – Collection Record Management
The system should manage and verify collection records.

## FR-03 – Salary Calculation
The system should calculate monthly farmer payments accurately.

## FR-04 – Deduction Management
The system should manage:
- Loan deductions
- Fertilizer deductions
- Tea deductions

## FR-05 – Receipt Generation
The system should generate printable receipts.

## FR-06 – Report Generation
The system should generate collection and payment reports.

## FR-07 – Route Synchronization
The system should synchronize mobile collection data with the office system.

## FR-08 – SMS Notifications
The system should support farmer collection notifications.

## FR-09 – Data Validation
The system should reduce duplicate entries and calculation errors.

---

# Salary Calculation Requirements

The system should calculate payments using:
- Tea collection totals
- Monthly tea rates
- Deduction records

Expected features:
- Automatic calculations
- Deduction summaries
- Payment history tracking
- Error reduction

---

# Receipt Requirements

Receipts should include:
- Farmer details
- Collection totals
- Deduction summaries
- Final payment amount
- Payment date

---

# Report Requirements

| Report Type | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Daily Collection Report | Monitor daily collections |
| Monthly Payment Report | Review payments |
| Loan Deduction Report | Track loan balances |
| Route Report | Monitor route performance |

- Payment tracking

- Business reporting

TeaRoutePay will modernize office operations by improving accounting accuracy, synchronization, reporting, and payment management while reducing manual processing tasks.
